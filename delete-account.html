Delete your HomeMate account

Last updated: 17 September 2026

HomeMate is published by Axpertz Online Connect LLP. You can delete your HomeMate account and its data in the app, or by email if you no longer have the app.

1. In the app

1. Open HomeMate and sign in with your Google account.
2. Open Profile.
3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.
Your account is deleted straight away. This cannot be undone.

2. Without the app

Send an email to cacssuraj78@gmail.com from the email address of the Google account you use in HomeMate, with the subject "Delete my HomeMate account". We use that address to find your account, so we cannot act on requests sent from a different address. Your account and its data are deleted within 30 days, and we reply when it is done.

3. What is deleted

- Your profile: name, email, mobile number, PIN code, area and profile photo.
- Your provider listing and its photos, if you published one.
- Callback requests.
- The call records you made to providers (your name, area and mobile number).
- The reports you made about listings.
- Your Google sign-in link to HomeMate and the data saved on your phone.

4. What is kept

Report counts on listings are kept only as anonymous numbers, so a listing that was reported keeps its count. They are not linked to you. Nothing else is kept, except where the law requires us to keep it.
