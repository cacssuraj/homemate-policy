HomeMate – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

This policy explains what HomeMate collects and what it does with it.

Who is responsible for your data

HomeMate is published by Axpertz Online Connect LLP (Axpertz) at block no 14/7 Gondegaon Colony, Kanhan, Nagpur, Maharashtra 441404, India. Write to us at cacssuraj78@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

- Your name and email from the Google account you sign in with.
- A phone number, photo, PIN code and area only if you enter them.
- If you publish a provider listing: your name, phone number, email, service, skills, experience, work area and photo.
- Which listings you called or reported, so the app can show your own history.

2. Why we collect it

To show providers near your area, to let you call them, and to show a provider how many calls and reports their listing received.

3. What other people see

A provider listing - name, phone number, service, skills, experience, area and photo - is public to every signed-in user of the app; that is the point of a listing. When you call a provider, your name and area are shown to that provider so they know who rang. Users browsing the app never see your phone number.

4. Where it is stored

In Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) and on your own phone. We do not sell your data, we never give your name, phone number or listing to advertisers, and we share it only where the law requires.

5. Ads

HomeMate is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: ad cards in some lists and small banners at the end of some screens. To show and measure these ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect your phone's advertising ID, your approximate location from your IP address, and information about how ads are seen and tapped, along with crash and diagnostic data. Google uses this under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

6. Payments

HomeMate never asks for and never stores card, bank or UPI details.

7. Your choices

You can edit your profile, change or delete saved locations, delete your provider listing from the dashboard, and sign out - signing out leaves your data on the phone so it is there when you sign back in. To delete your account and everything in it, open Profile and tap Delete my account; it is removed straight away and cannot be undone. Data belonging to another account is removed from a phone as soon as a different account signs in on it.

8. Children

The app is not meant for anyone under 18.
