HomeMate – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

This policy explains what HomeMate collects, why, who can see it, and how you can delete it.

Who is responsible for your data

HomeMate is published by Axpertz Online Connect LLP (Axpertz) at block no 14/7 Gondegaon Colony, Kanhan, Nagpur, Maharashtra 441404, India. Write to us at cacssuraj78@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

- Your name and email address from the Google account you sign in with. Google sign-in is the only way to sign in.
- A mobile number and a profile photo, only if you add them.
- The PIN code and area you type in to choose your location. HomeMate does not use your phone's GPS and does not ask for location permission.
- If you publish a provider listing: your name, phone number, email, service, skills, experience, work area and photo.
- When you call a provider: a call record with your name, area and mobile number (one record per provider per day).
- The reports you make about listings.
- Advertising and diagnostic data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (see section 5).

2. Why we collect it

To sign you in, show providers near the area you chose, let you call a provider and let that provider call you back, show a provider who called and how many reports their listing received, keep fake listings off the app, and show the ads that keep HomeMate free.

3. What other people see

- A provider listing (name, phone number, service, skills, experience, area and photo) is visible to every signed-in user of the app; that is the point of a listing.
- When you call a provider, your name, area and mobile number are visible only to that provider (and to HomeMate for moderation), so they know who rang and can call you back. Other users never see your mobile number.
- A report is visible only to the person who made it, the owner of the listing and HomeMate. Other users see only the number of reports.

4. Where it is stored

In Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which processes the data for us, and on your own phone. Photos are stored as small images in Cloud Firestore. Data is encrypted in transit. We do not sell your data, we never give your name, phone number or listing to advertisers, and we share it only with these service providers or where the law requires.

5. Ads

HomeMate is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: ad cards marked "Ad" in some lists and small banners at the end of some screens. To show and measure these ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects your phone's advertising ID, your approximate location from your IP address, information about how ads are seen and tapped, and crash and diagnostic data, and shares them with Google. Google uses this under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services (https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads). You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

6. PIN code lookup

When you type a PIN code, only that PIN code is sent to the India Post PIN code service (api.postalpincode.in) to find the area names.

7. Payments

HomeMate never asks for and never stores card, bank or UPI details.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can edit your profile, change or delete saved locations, delete your provider listing from the dashboard, and sign out - signing out leaves your data on the phone so it is there when you sign back in.
To delete your account in the app, open Profile and tap Delete my account. This straight away removes your profile, your provider listing, your photos, callback requests, the call records you made, the reports you made (each also comes off that listing's report count), and the data saved on your phone. It cannot be undone. If users reported your provider listing, those reports are kept with the listing's mobile number and email, so that number and email cannot be listed again until HomeMate approves a request.
If you no longer have the app, ask for deletion here: https://cacssuraj.github.io/homemate-policy/delete-account.html. We delete the account within 30 days.
Data belonging to another account is removed from a phone as soon as a different account signs in on it.

9. Children

HomeMate is only for people aged 18 or older. It is not meant for children.
